# DFW Coffee Shop Leads (verified 2026-07-31)

Every lead fact-checked against a live URL by an independent verification pass. Angles: wholesale carry / guest-roaster feature / gifting partner / relationship-only.

## Edison Coffee Co — Lewisville, TX
- **Address:** Roastery and Training Lab, Lewisville TX (street address not published on site; open Mon-Fri 9am-5pm)
- **Angle:** relationship-only
- **Why fit:** Lewisville's own specialty roaster, literally in Berrova's hometown. Their site sells single origins from Colombia, Ethiopia, and Kenya with tasting notes, pushes direct producer relationships and traceability (they tell the story of a specific Colombian farm restoration), and runs a wholesale program that supplies other DFW cafes including George in Coppell. Traceability is their brand language, which is exactly Berrova's JACRA-certification pitch.
- **Evidence:** https://edisoncoffeeco.com/
