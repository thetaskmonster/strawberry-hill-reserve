# Cold Intro Drafts — DFW Coffee Shops (2026-07-31)

Drafts only. Kyle reviews, personalizes if he wants, and sends every one himself.
Rules baked in: no sample DATE promised (the offer is "the moment my first
shipment lands, I'll bring you one in person"), no traction claims, no invented
urgency, nothing about other shops. Follow-up rule: no reply in 4-5 days = walk
in per the visit kit, don't re-email.

## Who gets the cold wave, who gets a walk-in instead

**Cold wave (16):** Perc, Trio, George, Main Line, Landmark, Lemma (Frisco,
covers all 3 locations), White Box, Coffee Del Rey, Filtered, Pax & Beneficia,
Liberation, Parks, Wayward, Houndstooth, La Reunion, Magnolias.

**Walk-in first, no cold email (6):** Edison, West Oak, Buon Giorno, Merit,
Oak Cliff, Ascension. These are roasters/credibility nodes where the strategy
is founder-to-founder respect; a cold pitch email undercuts it. Visit as a
customer per the visit kit.

## The master template (adapt per shop with the opener lines below)

> **Subject: Certified Jamaica Blue Mountain — local founder, quick question**
>
> Hi [name / team],
>
> [PERSONALIZED OPENER — one sentence proving I know their shop.]
>
> I'm Kyle, in Lewisville. I'm launching Berrova on September 1 — a certified
> Jamaica Blue Mountain line called Strawberry Hill Reserve. The short version:
> most "Blue Mountain" sold in the US is blended or faked, and ours carries
> JACRA certification, the Jamaican authority's mark for the real thing.
>
> I'm not selling anything today. My first shipment is on its way from Jamaica,
> and the moment it lands I'd like to drop off a sample in person — no pitch,
> just the coffee and the certification paperwork, and you tell me honestly
> what you think. [ANGLE LINE.]
>
> Worth five minutes? Who's the right person to bring it to?
>
> Kyle Ward-Chalk
> Berrova — berrova.com

**Angle lines:**
- *Guest-roaster feature:* "If it earns a slot in your rotation someday, great — but the sample comes with zero strings."
- *Wholesale carry:* "If a certified single-origin ever makes sense on your shelf, I'd rather you judge it in the cup first."
- *Gifting partner:* "I think it may fit the gift-ready side of what you do — but taste first, talk later."

**IG DM variant (when no email exists):**
> Local founder in Lewisville — launching a JACRA-certified Jamaica Blue
> Mountain line Sept 1. Most "Blue Mountain" in the US is faked; ours is
> certified, and I'd love to drop off a sample in person when my first
> shipment lands. No pitch, just the coffee. Who should I bring it to?

## After sending

Log each send in the pipeline (shop, channel, date). Reply → book the drop-off.
No reply in 4-5 days → walk in as a customer per the visit kit and mention the
note ("I emailed last week — figured I'd come taste the competition instead").
Every drop-off ends with one question: "who decides on new beans, and when
should I follow up?"
